<section class="page-section page-section--surface" id="research" aria-labelledby="research-title">
  <div class="section-shell">
    <header class="section-intro" data-reveal>
      <div>
        <span class="section-label">01 · Research</span>
        <h2 class="visually-hidden" id="research-title">Research</h2>
      </div>
      <p class="section-lead">
        My research themes are closely connected: high spatial resolution reveals small
        structures, motion correction preserves that detail, and quantitative tools turn
        vessel and neurofluid images into measures of physiology and pathology.
      </p>
    </header>

    <div class="research-grid">
      <article class="research-card" data-reveal>
        <div class="research-card__image">
          <img
            src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/hires/T1wDivT2w.gif"
            alt="Ultra-high-resolution structural brain MRI comparison"
            width="379"
            height="308"
            loading="lazy"
            decoding="async">
        </div>
        <div class="research-card__body">
          <span class="card-index">01</span>
          <h3>Ultra-high-field and mesoscopic MRI</h3>
          <p>
            I use 7T MRI to image the human brain at the mesoscopic scale and have acquired
            in-vivo images with isotropic resolution down to 140&nbsp;µm.
          </p>
          <ul class="tag-list" aria-label="Topics">
            <li>7T MRI</li>
            <li>High resolution</li>
            <li>Image contrast</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>

      <article class="research-card" data-reveal>
        <div class="research-card__image">
          <img
            src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/moco/MPTMotionCorrectionPrinciple.gif"
            alt="Principle of prospective MRI motion tracking and correction"
            width="1134"
            height="514"
            loading="lazy"
            decoding="async">
        </div>
        <div class="research-card__body">
          <span class="card-index">02</span>
          <h3>Motion correction</h3>
          <p>
            High-resolution imaging succeeds only when small head movements can be measured
            and corrected with comparable precision.
          </p>
          <ul class="tag-list" aria-label="Topics">
            <li>Prospective correction</li>
            <li>Tracking</li>
            <li>Image fidelity</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>

      <article class="research-card" data-reveal>
        <div class="research-card__image">
          <img
            src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/neurofluid_img/HiResToFQSM.png"
            alt="High-resolution arterial angiography and QSM-derived cerebral venography"
            width="926"
            height="609"
            loading="lazy"
            decoding="async">
        </div>
        <div class="research-card__body">
          <span class="card-index">03</span>
          <h3>Neurofluid imaging</h3>
          <p>
            MRI offers a non-invasive view of arteries, veins, perivascular spaces, and other
            fluid-filled compartments. I study their structure and function, and their links
            to aging, vascular health, and neurological disease.
          </p>
          <ul class="tag-list" aria-label="Topics">
            <li>Vessels</li>
            <li>Perivascular spaces</li>
            <li>Brain clearance</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>

      <article class="research-card" data-reveal>
        <div class="research-card__image">
          <img
            src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/vdm/VDM-pipeline_simple.png"
            alt="Vessel distance mapping workflow from MRI to segmentation and distance map"
            width="935"
            height="429"
            loading="lazy"
            decoding="async">
        </div>
        <div class="research-card__body">
          <span class="card-index">04</span>
          <h3>Vessel distance mapping</h3>
          <p>
            Vessel distance mapping connects segmented vascular networks with the surrounding
            anatomy. The framework supports quantitative analysis of vessel patterns in MRI
            and has also been applied to microscopy data.
          </p>
          <ul class="tag-list" aria-label="Topics">
            <li>Segmentation</li>
            <li>Quantification</li>
            <li>Open tools</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>
    </div>
